## Positioning
Media company built around Yolando Mitchell Brown's shows, studio, and teaching. Five brands + the founder, each with its own handle.

## Brands / sections (in page order)

1. **Every Way Woman** — founded 2004. A talk show created by women: real women having real conversations and finding real solutions on the issues that matter most, with co-hosts. Embraces viewpoints from women of all colors, creeds, political affiliations, and religious backgrounds. Handle: @everywaywoman

2. **InaviZion Media** — founded 2015. Born from a personal need for creative space and a passion for supporting fellow artists. An affordable, inspiring studio environment for filmmakers, photographers, podcasters, artists — fostering innovation, collaboration, artistic growth. Handle: @inavizionmedia

3. **Talk Show Land** — founded 2018 by Yolando Mitchell Brown. Daytime TV destination: news, talk, entertainment. Continues the legacy of Every Way Woman and The Movement. Handle: @talkshowland

4. **My Creative Space** — founded 2019, Burbank CA. Hub for creative entrepreneurs: affordable, versatile space for workshops, classes, events, podcasts, film productions. Handle: @ineedmycreativespace

5. **Start The Possible** — real-time business coaching for beginners. No fluff, just action: hands-on live training, practical no-nonsense guidance, no pre-recorded fluff, no upsells. Handle: @startthepossible

6. **Yolando Mitchell Brown** — founder. Coach, business owner, producer, teacher, visionary at the intersection of creativity, tech, and community. Launched a talk show for women from her living room; when she lost her studio space she opened her own. Passion for tech and reading; teaches entrepreneurs to start without overwhelm. Mission: break down barriers, make dreams accessible, turn ideas into reality. Handle: @yolandomitchellbrown
